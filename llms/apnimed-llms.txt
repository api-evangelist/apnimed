# Apnimed

> Apnimed is a late-stage clinical-stage pharmaceutical company headquartered in Cambridge,
> Massachusetts, founded in 2017 to develop novel oral drug therapies for sleep-related
> breathing disorders. Its lead candidate AD109 (Oxnimbi) is a fixed-dose combination of an
> anti-muscarinic and a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor that improves upper-airway
> muscle activity, aimed at an underlying neuromuscular cause of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA)
> rather than mechanical management. Apnimed partners with Shionogi through the
> Shionogi-Apnimed Sleep Science (SASS) joint venture, and its New Drug Application for AD109
> was accepted by the FDA in 2026.
